Small Family Matters

Episodes from family life

Every family runs on small matters. Who left the shirt on the chair. Whether the fan was switched off. How many kilos a mother may pack for children in a hostel. Why an elder refused the prasad. What the neighbours were told, and by whom.

None of these is important on its own, and yet together they are the real fabric of family life: the rules that hold a home together, the faith that steadies it, the food that carries love across a thousand kilometres, the gentle disagreements that nobody ever quite admits losing. The episodes follow one household across three generations: a time when the men ate first and a wife never called her husband by his name, a long marriage of instructions given once and remembered late, and a new kitchen where the son cooks between his mother on the phone and his wife beside him. Somewhere along the way one question keeps returning: are the men of this family progressing, or not?

The people, places and events in these pages are entirely imagined, though families everywhere may find something familiar in them.
