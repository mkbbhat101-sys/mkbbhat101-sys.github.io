Small Family Matters

Episodes from family life

Shobha never taught in a school, but she had the gift all the same. Ramesh is her only student. He enrolled, as far as he can remember, on the day they married, and has never quite managed to graduate.

These are gentle, humorous stories from an ordinary home: the shirt left on the dining chair, the exhaust fan that keeps talking, the suitcase three kilos over the limit, and the rebellions a husband plans on his morning walk and abandons before he reaches the door. The characters and places are fictional, though anyone who has lived in a family may recognise them.
