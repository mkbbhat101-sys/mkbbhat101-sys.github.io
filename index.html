“Some stories deserve to be remembered—not because they are extraordinary, but because they are real.”

I was born in Chandpora, Harwan, in Kashmir, and left the Valley with my family in 1990. I went on to spend 34 years in government accounting service with the Defence Accounts Department, working across India. Now based in Bengaluru, I am writing, learning, and contributing to the preservation of Kashmir’s cultural heritage.

This website brings together the different parts of my journey: my books, my professional experience in public finance and accounting, and my continuing work in learning and governance.

Memoir • Volume I in final stages

Kashmir and Beyond

A deeply personal account of family, memory, resilience, and the events that transformed ordinary lives. Through the metaphor of the Four Houses, it traces a journey from the orchards of Chandpora, Harwan, to rebuilding life in Delhi, professional service across India, and retirement in Bengaluru.

This work is not intended to revisit old divisions, but to preserve memories with honesty, dignity, and compassion.
