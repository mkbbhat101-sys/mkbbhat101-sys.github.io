Kashmir and Beyond

“Some stories deserve to be remembered—not because they are extraordinary, but because they are real.”

This memoir is a deeply personal account of family, memory, resilience, and the events that transformed ordinary lives. It draws on my experiences and memories, reflecting individual journeys within a shared history.

This work is not intended to revisit old divisions, but to preserve memories with honesty, dignity, and compassion.

What it’s about

At its heart, this is a book about memory—what we choose to carry forward, and what it means to hold an experience with honesty rather than nostalgia or grievance. It is written from a personal vantage point, but reaches toward something larger than one life: a shared history, seen through individual eyes.

Topics explored

  • Family, memory, and how experiences are carried across a lifetime
  • Resilience through change and hardship
  • Individual journeys set within a shared, lived history
  • Preserving memory with honesty, dignity, and compassion—without revisiting old divisions

The manuscript is currently in progress. Selected excerpts will be shared here as it moves toward publication.
